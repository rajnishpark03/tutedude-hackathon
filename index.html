What you submit

Three deliverables. One package.

Every team turns in all three. Each one is judged by a different criterion, so a missing piece costs you a third of the score.

01 · Product

Core software solution & UX

A functional product addressing the selected challenge prompt, backed by intuitive UI/UX design.

  • Working build, deployed or runnable
  • Source repository with real commit history
  • Clear flow for the core user task
02 · Web

Web experience overhaul

A redesigned, modern web interface demonstrating seamless interaction and visual polish.

  • Responsive on phone and desktop
  • Consistent branding and assets
  • Hosted link submitted before the deadline
03 · Media

Promo video

A high-impact promotional video that concisely communicates the value proposition, features and target-audience impact.

  • Short and punchy
  • Shows the real product in use
  • Video file timestamped before the deadline

Deadline rule: all repositories, hosted assets and video files must be timestamped before the 24-hour sprint deadline expires. Late uploads aren't judged.

What is the TuteDude Internal Hackathon? +

An intensive 24-hour innovation sprint exclusively for the TuteDude learning ecosystem. Teams build a product, a web experience and a promo video around a problem statement revealed at the launch ceremony.

Who can participate? +

Only TuteDude users: anyone currently enrolled in any active TuteDude course. Outside individuals, alumni and non-enrolled participants are not permitted.

What happens if someone on our team isn't a TuteDude student? +

The whole team is disqualified, not just that member. This applies at registration, during the 24-hour sprint and at judging. Every member must be a currently enrolled TuteDude student.

Can I participate solo? +

No. Solo submissions aren't accepted. Every participant must be registered as part of an official team of 3 to 5 members to submit and qualify for judging.

Can my team mix students from different courses? +

Yes, and it's recommended. Teams that cover design, engineering and marketing tend to finish with a complete package.

How do I find teammates? +

Use the SpeakUp global channel inside your TuteDude account, where every learner appears under their real name. Post what you need, reply to others, and build the team yourself.

When do we get the problem statement? +

At the launch ceremony on 4 November. The opening session introduces the challenge themes and reveals the problem statements publicly.

When does the 24-hour timer start? +

Immediately after the launch ceremony concludes. From that moment, teams have exactly 24 consecutive hours to ideate, build, test and package their submission.

What exactly do we submit? +

Three things: the core software solution with its UX, a redesigned modern web interface, and a promotional video. All repositories, hosted assets and video files must be timestamped before the deadline.

How is the winner chosen? +

Teams are ranked on product design, technical execution and campaign presentation. The full ₹20,000 pool goes to the single top-ranking team.

Is there an entry fee? +

No. Registration and participation are free for TuteDude users.

Where will updates be posted? +

In the SpeakUp channel: the ceremony link, problem statements, submission instructions and the winner announcement all land there.
