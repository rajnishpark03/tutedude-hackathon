Why a Buildathon?

Because learning alone is hard.

  • 🤝Meet learners beyond your course.
  • 🔁Learn from each other: design, code, marketing, video.
  • 📚Study together on TuteDude Sessions.
  • 🚀Build proof: a real product and a certificate.

The prize is the bonus. The real win is your team.

What can we win? +

🥇 ₹20,000, 🥈 ₹6,000 and 🥉 ₹4,000 for the top 3 teams. Everyone gets a certificate.

Who can take part? +

Any TuteDude student, from any course. We check each email with TuteDude. It's free.

How do we register as a team? +

The leader taps “Register your team”, adds their details and each teammate's name, WhatsApp and email, and watches a 1-minute video. Done.

Do my teammates need to register too? +

No. They can open the team link to see the team page and get updates.

I don't have a team. +

Come to a Team Building Session, every morning and evening on TuteDude Sessions, form a team, then register.

What will we build? +

On 21 Nov, 7 PM a real problem appears on your team page. Build a novel solution: the product, its website and a short promo video.

Can I switch teams? +

Yes, until teams lock on 20 Nov, 11:59 PM. Leave your team, then open the other team's link.

Who can see my WhatsApp number? +

Only your teammates and the organisers.
