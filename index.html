Rules

Short and simple.

  • TuteDude students only. One outsider on a team and the whole team is disqualified.
  • Pick a team that needs you. When you register you see only the teams that still need your skill.
  • One person per skill. Every team gets a mix: designer, developer, marketer, video editor.
  • Up to 4 people. A team of 4 completes on its own. The leader can also complete a team of 2 or 3.
  • TuteDude Sessions first, then WhatsApp. Agree to do your product discussion on a TuteDude session and your teammates' WhatsApp numbers unlock. Only your teammates and the organisers see your number.
  • Free. No fee, no payment details.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Still stuck? Ask in TuteDude Community.

How do I get a team? +

Register and choose your skill. You'll see the teams that still need someone like you; pick one and you're in. If no team needs your skill right now, you start a new team, become its leader and give it a name. The next people to register fill it.

Can I see the other teams? +

Only while registering, and only the names of teams that need your skill. After that you see just your own team, so everyone can focus on their teammates.

I'm alone in my team. What now? +

New people join as they register and show up on your team page. Share the Buildathon link with friends, and turn on notifications so you know the moment someone joins.

Why do I have to agree to TuteDude Sessions? +

A TuteDude session is a live room where your team meets, discusses the product and builds. Everyone watches the 1-minute video on their team page till the end and agrees to do the product discussion there. That unlocks your teammates' WhatsApp numbers.

How do I create the session? +

On TuteDude, open any lecture → Community → Sessions. Create a room, pick “Doubt or study”, write your team name + “Buildathon”, set the audience to “Everyone” and tap Create room. Your teammates find it in Sessions by your team name. Your team page has a 1-minute video too.

Will I get notifications? +

Yes, tap “Turn on notifications” after you register. You'll get one when someone joins your team, before teams lock, and when the problem statement is out. On iPhone, first add this site to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

Who can see my WhatsApp number? +

Only your own teammates (after they agree to meet on a TuteDude session) and the organisers. Nobody else.

Can I switch teams later? +

No. Once you join a team you stay in it, so pick carefully when you register. If a teammate never responds, your leader can move them out and the next person with that skill joins.
